# Task 2: Phishing Awareness Training

Part of my CodeAlpha Cyber Security Internship. This is a training presentation
that teaches individuals and company staff how to recognise, avoid and report
phishing attacks.

## Who it's for
Everyday individuals and company staff. No technical background needed.

## What the training covers
1. What phishing is and why it works (it targets people, not machines)
2. How an attack works: bait, hook, action
3. Types of phishing: email, spear phishing, smishing, vishing,
   business email compromise and fake websites
4. Social engineering tactics: urgency, fear, authority, greed or
   curiosity, and trust
5. How to spot a phishing email and a fake website
6. Real-world cases: Twitter (2020), Google and Facebook (2013-2015), MGM Resorts (2023)
7. What to do if you already clicked
8. Best practices for individuals and for company staff
9. A 6-question quiz with explained answers

## Key lessons
- Slow down: urgency is the attacker's best tool
- Verify the sender, the link and the request, and confirm money requests
  through a second channel
- A padlock (HTTPS) means the connection is encrypted, not that the site is honest
- Report quickly: tell IT or your bank straight away
